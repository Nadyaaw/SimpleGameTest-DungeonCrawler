📖 How to Play - Dungeon Explorer

1. Dungeon Exploration & Routes

Navigate through the dungeon by choosing Go Left (←), Go Forward (↑), or Go Right (→). Every choice presents random encounters: Monster Rooms (at least 3 enemies), Elite Rooms, Boss Rooms, Treasure/Trap Chests, Mysterious Merchants, and Quests!

2. Turn-Based Combat & Skills

Damage calculation: Player ATK - Enemy DEF (minimum 1). Unlock Fire Strike at Level 3 (deals 2x ATK damage, 3 turns cooldown). Open inventory during battle to equip gear or use healing potions!

3. Leveling System & Stat Upgrades

Defeating enemies grants EXP. Each level requires more EXP (scaled). On leveling up, your HP is fully restored and you choose between +5 ATK, +3 DEF, or +20 MAX HP.

4. Level Milestone Reward

Every 10 levels (Level 10, 20, 30, 40...), you receive a Milestone Reward containing 1 free random equipment of random rarity!

5. Item Rarity & Shops

Rarities: Common (55%), Uncommon (25%), Rare (13%), Epic (6%), Legendary (1%). Buy and sell items at the Town Shop (50% sell price) or find the Mysterious Merchant in dungeons for 50–70% discounts!

6. Death & Save System

If HP reaches 0, you awaken in town without losing any gold, inventory, or equipped items. In Progresss is saved automatically in your browser's localStorage!
